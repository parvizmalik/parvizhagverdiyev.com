I’m Parviz. Software engineer in telecom, based in Baku.

Knowing things halfway drives me nuts. If I use a tool, I need to know what’s going on under the hood. That’s how I ended up deep in GPU matrix math, how CPUs talk to devices, and what an LLM actually does with my prompt. One question opens three more, and I keep digging until it clicks.

I learn a bit every single day, usually after work: Kubernetes, Linux, AI. Some days nothing makes sense. I show up anyway.

Same energy at the gym. I lift, I hit the heavy bag, I run. It’s my reset button. I’m not chasing medals. I just want to be strong, fit, and able to throw hands.

I think in three languages: Azerbaijani, Russian, English. Ideas land in one and come out in another.

This blog is where I write down what I learn in plain words. If I can’t say it simply, I’m not done digging yet.
