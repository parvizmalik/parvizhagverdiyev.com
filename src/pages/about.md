I’m Parviz. Software engineer in telecom, based in Baku.

Knowing things halfway drives me nuts. If something grabs my attention, I read the papers and the articles, and I keep digging until it clicks. One week it’s GPU kernels, the next it’s how a CPU talks to hardware, then a fresh AI paper. I’m not picky about the topic. I just need to understand it all the way down.

I learn something every single day. Some days nothing makes sense. I show up anyway.

This blog is where I break it all down in plain words. If I can’t say it simply, I’m not done digging yet.
